My Dearest Vidhu,

I have been trying to write this for weeks. Not because I didn't know what to say but because everything I typed felt too small. Too thin. Like trying to pour the ocean into a glass and calling it the sea.

So let me try differently. Let me be honest in the way I only ever am with you.

There are people who enter your life and leave fingerprints on the surface of it. And then there are people who reach somewhere much deeper into the parts of you that you yourself were still figuring out and they hold those parts steady. You are that person for me, Vidhu. You didn't just see me. You saw through me. And you stayed.

You reached my subconscious. Do you understand how rare that is? Most people can't even reach past the version of me I perform for the world. You skipped all of that entirely. You walked straight past every wall I'd built and sat down inside me like you'd always been there like you belonged there. Because you do.

The man I am right now the one who stands a little taller, thinks a little deeper, loves a little braver that man has your fingerprints all over him. Not because you fixed something broken. But because you reflected something back to me I couldn't see on my own.

I think about the distance between us some nights and I don't feel sad anymore. I feel grateful. Because every call, every night you fell asleep on the phone, every stupid laugh at 2am it means we chose each other in the hardest possible conditions. Without convenience. Without proximity. Just purely, stubbornly, us.

That's not love by default. That's love by decision. Every single day.

I want to sit across from you at a table in some city neither of us has been to yet, and watch your eyes light up at something new and think: this. this is what I was building toward. this is what all the waiting was for.

Happy Birthday, Vidhu. You are the most beautiful disruption that ever happened to my life. Keep manifesting. Keep being exactly, entirely, unapologetically you. The world my world is so much better because you exist in it. 💛

Yours, in every universe ♥
